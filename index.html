Step-by-Step Pairing Guide

Follow these 4 simple steps to connect your WaveBird to the WavePhoenix receiver.

1

Plug Receiver into Controller Port

Insert your WavePhoenix receiver module firmly into Controller Port 1 (or Ports 2–4 for additional players) on your GameCube, Wii, or USB Adapter.

💡 Tip: Ensure the 6-pin connector is aligned properly before pushing it all the way into the port.
2

Press Front Pairing Button

Press the circular button on the front face of your WavePhoenix receiver. The green LED light right below the button will start flashing rapidly.

⚡ Search Mode: Rapid blinking means the receiver is actively scanning for an incoming controller transmission.
3

Power ON Controller & Hold X + Y

Slide the power switch on your WaveBird controller to the ON position. Press and hold both the X and Y buttons simultaneously for 1–2 seconds.

🎮 Technique: Hold down X and Y firmly together until the receiver registers the signal.
4

Confirm Solid Green Light (Connected)

Once paired, the LED light on the front of the WavePhoenix receiver will stop flashing and turn Solid Green. You are ready to play!

✨ Success: Virtual Pairing automatically remembers your controller settings for future gameplay.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

Quick fixes for common connectivity questions.

Why does the receiver LED keep flashing rapidly?
The receiver timed out before receiving the X + Y signal. Make sure your WaveBird power switch is set to ON, press the front pairing button on WavePhoenix again, and hold X + Y for 2 seconds.
Does the channel dial on the WaveBird controller matter?
No! You can set the channel dial on your WaveBird controller to any number from 1 to 16. WavePhoenix's Virtual Pairing scans across all channels to find and lock onto your controller automatically.
Can I use 4 WavePhoenix receivers at the same time?
Yes! Plug receivers into Ports 1, 2, 3, and 4. Set each WaveBird controller to a different channel dial number (e.g. 1, 5, 9, 13) and pair each one individually using the button press + X + Y procedure.
What should I do if the receiver LED doesn't turn on at all?
Ensure the receiver is pushed firmly into the controller port. Verify that your GameCube or USB Adapter is receiving power.
